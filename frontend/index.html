Privacy Notice

Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

Who is responsible. The University of Zululand is the responsible party for the personal information you provide through this service.

What we collect. Your full name, student number, email address, campus, password (stored only as a secure hash), the proof of registration PDF (and ID PDF if you choose the online pathway), optionally proof of residence, and — if you choose the online pathway — a live selfie and a photograph of your ID for identity verification.

Why we collect it. To verify your registration, produce and issue your student card, confirm your booking, and — for the online pathway — to verify your identity online before your card is printed.

Who we share it with. Only the University's card services team, our identity verification provider (VerifyNow, who store temporary files in Cape Town, South Africa and do not retain reports after delivery), and where required by law, authorised University officials and regulators. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it. Documents are retained only as long as needed to issue your card and for the University's legally required record-keeping period, after which they are securely destroyed.

Your rights. Under POPIA you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. If a decision about you is made automatically, you have the right to request human review. Contact the Information Officer at information.officer@unizulu.ac.za.

Complaints. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforeg.org.za.
